## 1. Defined extension and passivity

The primary complex still has 13 vertices and 20 cells. Its mechanical surrogate has 43 spatial point masses (13 primary plus 30 housings), one scalar alpha and 150 relative device coordinates: **280 configuration coordinates / 560 mechanical phase variables**, plus a heat accumulator. The new loss adds no mass, storage or state. Neither 120 added dashpots nor 43 point masses is a simplex count.

For each original shell edge e=(i,j), let k,l be the opposite vertices of its two adjacent exterior faces. Connect its housing H_e to anchors a in {i,j,k,l} by four current-axis, central, viscous struts. For d_ea=H_e-X_a, n_ea=d_ea/|d_ea|, define

\[
 \mathcal R_h=\frac\eta2\sum_{e,a}[n_{ea}\cdot(\dot H_e-\dot X_a)]^2,
 \qquad \eta=\nu\frac{2\beta}{5R^2},\quad \nu\ge0.
\]

The coefficient is fixed from nominal mean housing inertia in each trajectory; mass scatter does not retune individual coefficients. Units are the parent's dimensionless conventions; physical damping coefficient is eta E_* t0/ell0². All 120 link lengths must remain positive.

For r_ea=n_ea·(V_e-v_a), housing force is -eta r_ea n_ea and anchor reaction is +eta r_ea n_ea. This gives exactly zero pair force and torque and power -eta r_ea². Together with the parent losses,

\[
 E'=-\frac\gamma{10}\|\dot z\|^2
     -\frac{c_f}{10}\sum_{\text{150 branches, two inputs}}\dot y_a^2
     -\eta\sum_{e,a}r_{ea}^2\le0.
\]

Total spatial linear and angular momenta include the housings and remain conserved. Primary momentum alone may change. Centrality uses the **current** d_ea, not a fixed reference direction; a reference-axis damper would not give this nonlinear torque identity.

This is a declared engineered surrogate with ideal massless, collision-free routing and ideal anchors. It supplies a mathematical passive loss mechanism, not evidence that 120 real dampers fit inside an icosahedron or are native UD dynamics. Housing-to-housing loss alone was rejected before the scan because it can miss relative rigid rotation of housing and primary groups.

## 2. Exact geometry and detection

At the reference geometry, each housing is A_e=(r_i+r_j)/2. Its four squared anchor lengths are (1/4,1/4,3/4,3/4) ell0². With Q(sqrt5) arithmetic, `exact.py` verifies all 30 housings independently. Every triple (d_ei,d_ek,d_el) has determinant ±1/4 ell0³, so its three directions span 3D.

The direction Gram S_e=sum_a n_ea n_ea^T has trace 4, trace(S_e²)=64/9 and determinant 8/9. Its characteristic polynomial is

\[
 (s-2)(s^2-2s+4/9),\qquad
 \operatorname{spec}S_e=\{2,(3-\sqrt5)/3,(3+\sqrt5)/3\}.
\]

For fixed primary velocities zero, reference housing power is therefore at least eta (3-sqrt5)/3 times sum_e |V_e|². The housing-only loss rank is 90, including every vector of the old 54-dimensional reaction-cancelling housing sector. In the housing mass metric, the instantaneous bound is at least nu (3-sqrt5)/(3 max w_e). **This is not a decay-rate bound for the coupled assembly:** primary motion can exchange energy and reduce relative velocity.

## 3. Conditional local linear decay, including c_f=0

Assume reference equilibrium, positive primary masses/g/k, positive housing masses and mount stiffnesses, positive device mu/kappa/gamma, positive reciprocal gains (1+delta), and nu>0. Input friction may be zero. The shell is the infinitesimally rigid icosahedral edge framework (exact edge rigidity rank30, six shell rigid motions). Conservative energy has precisely six global rigid zero modes; its other 274 configuration directions have positive stiffness.

An undamped positive-frequency mode of M q''+C q'+K q=0 must satisfy Cq=0. Device loss makes all z components zero. Four-anchor loss makes every axial relative velocity zero. Subtracting the two endpoint constraints of each housing shows (r_i-r_j)·(v_i-v_j)=0, so all shell velocities are a single infinitesimal rigid motion. Subtract that motion from the complete assembly. The shell anchors are now stationary, and the rank-three Gram at every housing forces all housing velocities to zero.

Only a relative centre velocity and alpha could remain. The z rows of the positive-frequency eigen-equation, with z=0, require Jq=0. Radial branch constraints say s_i'=(1+delta_ij)s_j'. Their graph is connected and every gain is positive. A nonzero solution would give all 12 s_i' the same sign. But stationary-shell radial rates generated by a moving centre satisfy sum_i s_i'=0 because sum_i r_i=0. Thus all s_i'=0 and the spanning radial directions force the centre velocity to zero.

Consequently the undamped mode lies in global rigid motions plus alpha. A positive-frequency mode is M-orthogonal to global rigid motions, so only alpha remains. K applied to a nonzero alpha has nonzero radial point components (-g_i r_i/R²); it cannot equal lambda M times the alpha-only vector. Contradiction. Hence there are no nonzero imaginary poles; all 274 nonrigid equilibrium coordinates decay in the linearization. The six global rigid coordinates and their momentum/drift partners retain their free-assembly meaning.

The argument uses the exact current model and positive gains; it does not supply a uniform numerical decay margin over a tolerance box, global nonlinear attraction, cell orientation preservation, or finite-amplitude breathing coherence. The nominal matrix spectrum is a numerical check of this proof, not its premise. The heat accumulator is bookkeeping, not another oscillatory mode.

## 4. Breathing cost and reduced equations

Icosahedral symmetry preserves the three-coordinate manifold alpha, primary scale b and housing scale c. The parent's M,K remain the same. The added reduced Rayleigh matrix is obtained from the actual anchor directions at (b,c), so it generally depends on those scales; `collective_loss` retains this dependence rather than extending a reference linear matrix to finite amplitude.

At b=c=0 the added b,c block is exactly

\[
 C_{h,bc}=\eta\begin{pmatrix}35&10\\10&5\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Under common homothety b=c, each link length scales uniformly and power is **60 eta b_dot²**. This dissipates common breathing. Passivity cannot be interpreted as exact preservation of the earlier undamped breathing trajectory. A finite housing lag also changes n_ea and must be included in nonlinear integration.

The design strength and screen are development choices from the nine-value [protocol](PROTOCOL.md). Worst housing ringdown uses a mass-weighted orthonormal basis of ker((N diag(m_h)) tensor I3) and **both** its displacement and velocity phases. Propagating all 108 normalized initial phases through the full linear system and taking the largest energy-Gram eigenvalue gives a numerical worst final energy fraction over that subspace, not a finite list of arbitrary directions.
