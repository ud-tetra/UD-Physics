# Multiport construction and design protocol v1.0

Development analysis, recorded before this candidate's computations. The previous geometry, soft modes, failed ordinary-dashpot screen and three property realizations are exposed. No independent predictive claim or empirical fitting.

Architecture: a reciprocal two-input differential transmission compares actual dimensionless strains on two members. Its output d=y_a-y_b feeds a spring of stiffness kappa/10 in series with a generalized auxiliary coordinate z carrying inertia mu/10 and viscous loss gamma/10. Input forces are the transpose of that same transmission. The scalar inertia represents a relative port/inertial element, not a point mass attached to fixed ground. Ideal transmission, routing, negligible common-port parasitics and linear component laws are explicit realization assumptions. The work is a complete passive port model; a manufactured linkage and its housing/routing masses are not thereby demonstrated.

Pair graphs:120 unordered pairs of shell edges sharing a vertex (line graph of the30-edge icosahedron), plus30 neighboring pairs of radial strains (icosahedral graph). Orient each pair by sorted input indices; z transforms with that orientation. There are150 auxiliary positions and150 auxiliary velocities,190 total configuration coordinates/380 phase coordinates. Positive mu,kappa,gamma; common normalization1/10 for every branch is a declared design choice.

1. Derive reciprocal input/output forces and Eprime=-gamma/10 sum zdot². Include all geometric point reactions and auxiliary modes. Prove zero common-strain output, unchanged homothetic breathing, exact network kernels and linear transverse asymptotic stability under default mechanical symmetry. Preserve the earlier ordinary-damper obstruction.
2. Exact integer graph connectivity/spectra and Q(sqrt5) derivative rank/kernel. Derive finite-frequency impedance including auxiliary inertia/compliance; do not replace it by an instantaneous projected dashpot.
3. Linear design grid: mu in{1/500,1/50,1/5}, kappa in{1/10,1/2,2}, gamma in{1/50,1/10,1/2},27 points. Maximize the smallest decay rate of the full transverse+auxiliary equilibrium generator; deterministic ties use grid order. This is a target-informed engineering selection. Record every point and the selected parameters.
4. Nonlinear campaign: selected design on the same3 stored ±5% property realizations and A0 in{1/40,1/10,1/5},9 points, compared with each matched undamped preparation. Report mean-volume RMS retention, hub and shell-centroid normalized volume mismatch, absolute shape RMS and hub-offset RMS. The illustrative screen is retention>=80%, hub-volume mismatch<=5%, and shape RMS<=50% of matched undamped; thresholds chosen before outputs. These are finite development probes, not tolerance-box bounds.
5. Three full symmetric checks at those amplitudes, plus common-input transmission-ratio errors at±0.1% and±1%, seed713, at A0=.1. Use the same ratio for sensing and reciprocal force return, so errors remain passive. Report breathing cost and symmetry leakage; do not call ideal common-mode cancellation robust to arbitrary transducer errors.
6. Verify force gradients by finite differences, force power by a separately assembled Jacobian, P/L and E+D balances. Refine the largest selected-network nonlinear mismatch. Sample oriented volumes and supporting halfspaces; retain the previous length-domain energy certificate without extending it to orientation or coherence.
