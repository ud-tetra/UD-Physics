## Mathematical contract

- Geometry: $a=\sqrt3(1.42)$ Å; bottom honeycomb basis $0,(a_1+a_2)/3$, top rotated by direct-coordinate matrix $R=(1/7)\begin{psmallmatrix}8&3\\-3&5\end{psmallmatrix}$; coincidence supercell columns $(2,1),(-1,3)$ with determinant 7 and exactly 14 upper atoms. Flat rigid sheets; bottom patch $p,q\in[-18,18]$ contains all pairs within the largest declared 18 Å spherical cutoff for sampled positions. No intralayer relaxation or dynamics.
- Pair potential in meV: $V=-A(z_0/r)^6+H(\rho)e^{-\lambda(r-z_0)}$ with the source's Table S1 coefficients. Set $D=A(z_0/r)^6$ and $R=H(\rho)e^{-\lambda(r-z_0)}$. For fixed projected $\rho$ and $r=\sqrt{\rho^2+z^2}$,
  $\partial_zV=6Dz/r^2-\lambda Rz/r$ and
  $\partial_z^2V=6D(1/r^2-8z^2/r^4)+R(\lambda^2z^2/r^2-\lambda\rho^2/r^3)$.
- Regress $E,E_z,E_{zz}$ separately on one intercept plus the same six first-star sine and cosine functions, at 16×17 sampled archived grid points. The fit records three individual wave amplitudes and directional log derivatives. Fixed-neighbor central differences verify the analytic pair derivatives. A cutoff variation is diagnostic, not an uncertainty interval.
- At the negative first-shell minimum $S=3$, $K_z^m=E_0''+3U_1''$ in meV Å$^{-2}$/atom. With $P$ in MPa, area per upper atom $\sigma=\sqrt3a^2/4$ Å² and $1$ eV Å$^{-3}=160217.6634$ MPa, $d_P\ln k_x=-[\sigma/(160217.6634 K_z^m[\mathrm{eV/Å^2}])](d_z\ln|U_1|)$, valid for the local equilibrium first-shell stiffness. The leading small-corrugation barrier has the same logarithmic slope; the exact relaxed saddle slope is the height envelope $z_s-z_m$.

## Numerical readout

At spherical cutoff 16 Å and $z=3.46$ Å, fitted $|U_1|=19.49$ μeV/upper atom and $d_z\ln|U_1|=-3.826$ Å$^{-1}$; $K_z^m=140.414$ meV Å$^{-2}$/atom. Conditional local stiffness slope $d_P\ln k_x=4.454\times10^{-4}$ MPa$^{-1}$. Leading barrier increment $0.03472$ μeV/atom/MPa and leading saddle-minimum height difference $0.002124$ Å. Changing the spherical cutoff to 14 or 18 Å gives $4.561\times10^{-4}$ or $4.501\times10^{-4}$ MPa$^{-1}$ within this reconstruction.

Against the deposited 3.46 Å energy grid, the 16 Å reconstruction has 0.99935 sampled spatial correlation after subtracting mean, a 1.292 μeV/atom centered RMS difference, a $-0.501$ meV/atom mean energy offset and 19.49 versus 20.25 μeV/atom first-star magnitude. This 3.8% corrugation difference blocks any claim of exact source simulation reproduction. The archived `(2,1)` folder says `10x10` whereas source Table I says `18x18`; that discrepancy remains unresolved. The plotted metadata Z-unit inconsistency is addressed in the earlier raw-grid capture. Alternate interlayer potentials give other responses; this cutoff sweep is not a physical error bar.
