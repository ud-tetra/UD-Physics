Archive is external source data, not committed wholesale; the verifier requires exact SHA to prevent unnoticed revisions. Run `python3 fit_minkin_raw.py /path/to/downloaded/archive.zip` with NumPy and SciPy. Only `X.txt`, `Y.txt`, and `Z.txt` under `data/pes21_10x10_Rc=16_lz=3.46` are parsed. No code from the archive is executed.

Grid: 128×129 at 3.46 Å. The deposited page says Z values are μeV, but its `Contour plot.m` multiplies the difference `Z-minZ` by 1000 before labeling it μeV; stored range 0.091043591 corresponds to 91.043591 μeV/upper atom. This is an internal source-unit discrepancy; absolute offset is irrelevant to the fit. The model uses physical X/Y in Å and the known first common reciprocal star $g_*=\sqrt7\,4\pi/(\sqrt3a)$, $a=\sqrt3(1.42)$ Å. Its three directed vectors at grid-frame polar angles 30°, 150°, 270° sum to zero.

The linear sine/cosine fit gives amplitudes 20.22665, 20.25023, 20.26591 μeV/atom and phase sum differing from π by 0.000247 rad. A three-cosine fit with a single negative coefficient and translation gives -20.24759 μeV/atom. First-shell RMS 0.94102 μeV/atom, or 1.0336% of the observed range, reproduces the published 1.04%. The next allowed reciprocal shell ($|g|^2/g_*^2=3$) has 0.670–0.705 μeV/atom fitted amplitudes and lowers interlaced holdout RMS from 0.94119 to 0.42330 μeV/atom, removing 79.8% of the first-shell squared remainder. Norm-4 and norm-7 shells improve it only to 0.42155 μeV/atom.

The holdout partitions pixels of one energy landscape, so it is not an independent experiment. This fixed-height PES cannot identify a normal derivative or damping. A finite spatial PES mismatch is a conventional model remainder, not a force residual or evidence of UD coupling. The remaining program gate is a height-resolved ordinary potential and a matched force-speed protocol, with source-derived UD weights fixed beforehand. The 2026 Chen *et al.* raw friction data are not present in this archive.
