# The index-seven conventional numerical benchmark

**Status:** NUMERICAL imported potential comparator; not independent experimental evidence, not a UD material law.

Source: Minkin *et al.*, *PRB* **108**, 085411 (2023), arXiv:2308.06302, Section III and Tables I–II, https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.06302 . Their deposited PES data are at https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/svnpzbjc3t/1 (DOI 10.17632/svnpzbjc3t.1); the files were not fetched or inspected here. Target: Chen *et al.*, *PRL* **137**, 166201 (2026), DOI 10.1103/ly48-b4l2, accessible abstract only; subscription full text and force curves unavailable.

At (2,1), $N_c=7$, 21.787°, separation 3.46 Å, their first-harmonic amplitude is negative $U_1=-2.02\times10^{-5}$ eV per atom of the upper graphene sheet. Their Eq. (3) has three cosines multiplied by $U_1$ per atom. Our Eq. (9) has the three cosines multiplied by $2V_*$ per two-atom upper Bravais cell: **$V_*=U_1$ numerically**, with different unit labels. The negative sign selects the single triangular minimum and the 8|V*| **per-cell** minimum sliding barrier. The conventional comparator gives 80.8 μeV/atom sliding barrier, 90.9 μeV/atom global range, 60.6 μeV/atom rotational mean barrier, 0.0018456 eV Å⁻² per atom curvature, about 9.14 cm⁻¹ shear mode, and 16.7 MPa ideal static shear strength. The published rotation barrier is 6.07×10⁻⁵ eV/atom, frequency 9.153 cm⁻¹ and shear strength 1.67×10⁷ Pa. Rounded input explains minor frequency difference.

The paper's first-shell RMS mismatch is 1.04% of its 90.9 μeV/atom range, roughly 0.95 μeV/atom. This is a conventional PES fit discrepancy, not a UD residual. Its alternate Lebedeva potential gives corrugation below numerical accuracy. Thus the benchmark is model-specific. The fixed-height PES has no vertical derivative or bath time. It cannot predict load-dependent friction by itself.

**Decision gate:** Source two or more independently computed height-resolved PES grids and a normal equation of state, record atom count and projected contact area, extract the force estimator at specified speed/temperature, and reserve measured Chen traces for held-out comparison. Fit ordinary potential choices and higher shells before any proposed UD residual. Select bath variables and seven orbit weights from a separate, physically mapped source and do not double count relaxed degrees of freedom. Physical promotion remains zero.
