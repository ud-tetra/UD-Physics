## Three scoped exact checks

**Reserve/anchor underdetermination.** On the same prepared bipyramid state
and the same old generator, the required obligation is (g=9/10).
The candidate predicate (F=R-g) equals zero for supplied (R=9/10) and
equals (1/10) for supplied (R=1). Q169 does not select between these
auxiliary reserves. Its zero state is stationary and has no reserve crossing
at (R=9/10). These examples do not invalidate the v0.1 local crossing
under its declared initial state/reserve; they prevent promoting that
conditional trigger to a universal source-selected event law.

**Sampling mesh underdetermination.** An exact trajectory
(c(u)=exp(Au)c(0)) of a fixed generator can be observed at
(u_j=j/2000) or at (u_j=j/100), with no change to the evolution or
conservation theorem. The first mesh satisfies the v0.6 ceiling
(1/1000), the second does not. A generator defines the trajectory
but does not select the acquisition mesh. A formal numerical experiment
can deliberately choose the fine mesh; that is a reproducible acquisition
design, not an autonomously derived observer/update law.

**Reference winding ambiguity.** For the v0.5 reference, phases
(u) and (u+2pi n/sqrt5) have identical reference readings.
The full branch's W has the same period, so whole cycles can remain hidden
even in the sampled pair. An unbounded inter-sample phase advance cannot
be bounded from these endpoint data alone. A phase-zero label is a legitimate
coordinate convention only AFTER an actual event/preparation anchor is
specified; relabeling time does not produce that event.

## Typed non-transfer

The filled K4 quadrature clock in the native-event source uses
(	heta=2s) on its active carrier. The bipyramid has different chain spaces,
generators and two boundary modes with squared frequencies 3 and 5 in the
present formal parameter. Their event counts, clock variables and recurrence
records are distinct objects. The QPB1 observer's four events per nonzero
quadrature pair do not imply a bound of 1/1000 on this protocol's phase
increment. Importing such a bound needs a declared intertwiner/clock map and
its actual domain, not matching labels or a generic event count.

## Disposition and reopening inputs

Preserve the proved conditional comparisons, calibration bounds, rational
classifiers, negative witnesses and immutable captures v0.1-v0.6.
Park the proposed use of this package as an AUTONOMOUS native startup
mechanism or as an independently ready physical experiment.

Reopen that promotion only with:
1. A source-defined state/preparation and move/actuator law that identifies
   the actual event, or an explicitly external preparation/event protocol.
2. A source-defined observation/update law with a proved accumulated phase
   bound per sample, or an explicit continuous no-missed-crossing contract.
3. A justified signed channel map and calibration/error receipts.

Items 1 and 2 are not supplied by the audited documents.
A designed emulator with explicit reset and native-phase sampling could
instantiate the mathematical protocol; it would test the declared emulator
laws and measurement chain, not establish that UD selected them.
