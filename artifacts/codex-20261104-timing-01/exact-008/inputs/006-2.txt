## Analytic obstruction on one filled tetrahedron

```text
u(s) = eps sin(2s)
f(s) = (eps/2) b cos(2s)
j(s) = u'(s) = 2eps cos(2s)
S = ||f||^2 + u^2 = eps^2
E = (j^2 + 4u^2)/2 = 2eps^2.
```

The remaining chain coordinates can be zero because B2 b=0. S is full support for this preparation; E is the reduced oscillator invariant, not SI energy. Their factor of two here does not identify them generally. The source parameter s is dimensionless.

Consequently, no finite function of positive conserved support alone can bound cumulative crossings for arbitrary duration on this branch. The same holds for its oscillator energy.
