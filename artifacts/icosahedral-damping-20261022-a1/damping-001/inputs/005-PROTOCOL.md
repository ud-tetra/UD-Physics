# Damping design protocol v1.0

Recorded before damping outcomes. Development data: previous free3D geometry, spectrum and ±5% disorder campaign are already exposed. This is target-informed engineering exploration, not an independent prediction.

Use the unchanged free3D potential/mass conventions at base c7d832c. Add independent central axial dashpots on12 radial strains s_i and30 ordinary edge strains e_ij: Rayleigh R=(c_s sum sdot_i^2+c_e sum edot_ij^2)/2. Uniform coefficients are dimensionless; u=t/t0, energy E_* remain uncalibrated. No preferred-radius actuator damping is added. Nonnegative coefficients are required. Include equal/opposite spatial reactions.

1. Prove passivity/momentum balances and derive the exact symmetric damped two-coordinate subsystem. Test whether common breathing can remain exactly protected by any nonzero coefficients.
2. Verify the soft tangential eigenspace over Q(sqrt5), then derive its decay law and compare with collective damping. Exact statements apply to the specified symmetric linearization; finite amplitude is a separate question.
3. Scan c_s in {0,1/200,1/50} and c_e=j/1000,j=0..120. Record mean-volume RMS retention over u0..100 from the exact invariant nonlinear collective solution at A0=1/10 and the soft modal exponential envelope at u100. Illustrative design screen: at least80% retained mean-volume RMS AND soft envelope at most50%. These are chosen engineering targets, not UD-derived thresholds. Report the sampled tradeoff even if no point passes; do not infer a continuous-parameter impossibility from a grid.
4. Nonlinear full3D campaign: A0 in {1/40,1/10,1/5}; coefficients (c_s,c_e)=(0,0),(0,1/200),(0,1/50),(1/50,1/50). Three ±5% property realizations: reuse previous sample7 (known largest exposed volume mismatch), plus two fresh deterministic points from seed10422. These are finite design probes, not box bounds or confidence intervals. Compare each damped point with its own matched undamped point. Report hub-relative and shell-centroid volume mismatch, absolute shape RMS, hub offset RMS and mean-volume RMS retention. Reusing worst case is deliberate target exposure.
5. Integrate cumulative dissipation as an extra state to verify E+D=E_initial, independently check force power and linearization, preserve P/L, and refine the largest observed damped volume mismatch. Check sampled oriented volumes/convexity. Do not infer all-time amplitude safety from samples.

Stop a proposed envelope from being called successful if either criterion fails. A failure can motivate a new multiport/difference damping candidate, which would require its own physical realization and test.
