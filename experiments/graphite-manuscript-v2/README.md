The revision keeps the conventional exact angle result `cos(theta)=13/14`, adds the N=7/13/19 quotient and local-source selection obstructions, and puts the proposed UD port after a full three-wave conventional passive drag control. Under the control's shared bath and per-atom contact assumptions, rounded published rigid Fourier coefficients give a **conditional low-speed** N=7/N=13 drag-coefficient ratio `651175/6877 ≈ 94.69`; an inequivalent one-coordinate static-shear transfer gives about 4.33. Neither is a measured kinetic friction ratio. The sixfold direction-dependent term first occurs at order `v^6` in dissipated power in the equal-channel control.

The UD candidate is `S(w)=M^T W B1^T`, `C=S(w)R`, `Gamma_port=S(w) L S(w)^T`, with `L=R K^-1 D_h K^-1 R^T` positive semidefinite. Uniform source weights cancel. An unrestricted passive bath can nevertheless realize any positive semidefinite two-dimensional tensor for a rank-two port; the topology does not select seven or identify a measured residual. A closed harmonic model has no Ohmic damping. The material source weights, internal complex loss, contact transfer, pressure derivatives and signed held-out force traces are missing.

The claim/data gate is to measure the full two-dimensional conservative PES and loss/contact response at the paired angles and controls, independently fix any UD source map and bath, and forecast the **signed** force and load response before examining held-out traces. A null or failed forecast narrows or rejects this material bridge. The accessible [Chen *et al.* PRL abstract](https://doi.org/10.1103/ly48-b4l2) reports detectable peaks and negligible load dependence, not friction identically zero. The [Lebedeva *et al.* DFT source](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.01338v1) supplies rigid PES coefficients, not kinetic force traces.
