## Independence classification

The estimator contains no hbar and must have no ancestor that inferred a calibration/preparation using A0=2*hbar or the candidate phase map. A calibration dependency graph makes declared dependencies auditable; it cannot establish that unrecorded dependencies do not exist.

The modern SI kilogram has a definition tied to h. Consequently target-independent inference with disclosed SI traceability differs from a strict claim that the full chain is h-free. This protocol supports explicit classification and reports the latter as unestablished when h-dependent standards occur. It does not amend the source programme's entry rules or certify metrological independence from metadata.
