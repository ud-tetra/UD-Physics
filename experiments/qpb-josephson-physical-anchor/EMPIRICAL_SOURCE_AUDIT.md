## Primary measurement

Wildermuth et al., *Fluxons in high-impedance long Josephson junctions*, Applied Physics Letters 120, 112601, published 14 March 2022, DOI https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0082197, third experiment and Data Availability. In a microwave-driven square-junction resonance experiment the authors jointly fit the DC-bias-dependent small-signal plasma frequency with the conventional formula f(gamma)=fp(1-gamma²)^(1/4), where gamma=Ib/Ic. They publish fp=13.28±0.05 GHz, Ic=8.36±0.08 microampere, and specific barrier capacitance c=36.4±0.04 fF/um². The published fitted estimates count as EMPIRICAL summary values. The raw data are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request, not acquired here. The fit covariance is not supplied in the retrieved accessible source.

## Dimensional calculation and circular-data boundary

The SI flux quantum Phi0=h/(2e), with defining h and e, and the ideal plasma identity imply

C = Ic/(2 pi Phi0 fp²) = 3.64850225477916 pF.

This is a **re-expression of the same joint fit**, not an independently measured C or a held-out prediction. Substituting printed marginal ± parameter ranges into this formula gives a rectangular sensitivity interval [3.586530509,3.711310154] pF. It is NOT a confidence interval. A hypothetical independent-errors linear propagation would give 0.04442727 pF, but this is not a valid uncertainty assignment to the joint fit without covariance. Dividing derived C by reported c suggests an effective area 100.2336 um², only conditionally if the values correspond to precisely the same junction; this is NOT independently verified geometry or validation of the model.

The published bias law can generate, for chosen gamma=0,0.2,0.4,0.6,0.8, *model-only* frequencies of 13.2800,13.1452,12.7136,11.8780,10.2866 GHz. These values are generated by a formula whose fp was fitted on the source data. They are not measurements and supply no residual. A DC bias gamma changes the equilibrium curvature and is NOT the zero-current-bias **turning phase amplitude a** of the elliptic-integral/AGM benchmark in jj-001. Replacing gamma by a would mix different physical objects and invalidate the comparison.

Source provenance correction: Phys. Rev. Research 2, 043091 (2020), DOI https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.2.043091, is *Phase-tunable thermoelectricity in a Josephson junction*. It includes the theoretical pendulum amplitude-frequency formula as Eq. 13. It is NOT an empirical zero-bias amplitude-frequency dataset.
