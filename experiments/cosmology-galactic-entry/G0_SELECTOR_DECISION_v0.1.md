## Lower-dependency entry: resolved nebular map topology

**RECOMMENDED CANDIDATE N0, mathematical comparator only:** topology of thresholded Hα emission *maps* at declared pixel/point-spread-function scale, such as connected component count and first Betti number across thresholds. Define mathematical input as a 2D intensity array with mask, noise and point-spread function; output as a dimensionless persistence summary on a fixed pixel adjacency convention. A native UD comparator would require a specified assembled carrier, evolution and scalar readout from its state to a 2D map or dimensionless excursion-set filtration. It must predict a distribution or trajectory without selecting thresholds after observing the target.

The ESO PHANGS–MUSE nebula DR1.1 catalog lists 30,790 reliable nebulae in 19 galaxies, based on Hα maps; its integrated nebula measurements are **not** themselves a resolved filament skeleton. ESO describes median spatial resolution around 70 pc. Source: ESO catalog `https://www.eso.org/qi/catalog/show/436` and release announcement `https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/announcements/sciann17705.html`, checked 2026-11-06. The original catalog paper warns that many individual regions are unresolved or marginally resolved and does not provide robust individual sizes as a primary column; use maps with resolution-aware controls, not fine topology of catalog objects. Source: Groves et al., MNRAS 520, 4902 (2023), §3.2 and size discussion. The comparison would initially be an image/topology bridge, **not** a claim about nebular chemistry, gravity or expansion.
