## Capture contract

- Route one coherent source through each detector's science and pilot paths. Record the actual routing, gain and aperture settings, instrument IDs, calibration standard, and trigger/timebase. Record a common-phase null at nominal and minimum declared signal levels, repeat with exchanged detector assignments, and record a known phase ramp of each sign with independent aperture timing markers. Capture gain drift over the relevant interval using an independent observation or a calibrated source path.
- Preserve raw complex quadratures and timestamps for both paths on both detectors. Each record needs acquisition ID, detector and path labels, acquisition/aperture timestamps and units, raw I/Q and units, source state, and validity flags. Preserve independent reference readings and their uncertainties. Attach a file manifest with SHA-256 digests and an operating-envelope description. Report dropped or invalid records and the predeclared rule governing them.
- From calibration, report lower science and pilot magnitude floors, upper complex additive error caps, equal-time residual path phase cap, maximum gain-phase slope, and science-to-pilot skew cap. Attach a traceable receipt for every bound, including the calibration standard and uncertainty, the applicable conditions, units, and whether its coverage is deterministic or statistical. Peak errors seen in finite captures do not establish deterministic bounds over unsampled conditions.
- Populate the JSON template only from measured or independently traceable evidence. Use rational strings or decimal strings with conservative rounding. Drift rate and skew must use the same timebase. Magnitudes and additive errors must use the same phasor scale. A signed differential test must retain a separately traceable lower bound on the absolute injected gap and a predeclared target. Physical hardware qualification for the proposed signal also requires a documented mapping of signal levels and timing to that operating envelope.
- Freeze the completed calibration packet and its hashes before the held-out controls in Bench acceptance v0.1. An arithmetic result alone does not accept the instrument.
