# A frozen signed-force test gate for the graphite bridge

**EXACT/CONDITIONAL statistics; empirical graphite residual OPEN.** The [working paper v3](../graphite-manuscript-v3/README.md) asks for a signed, held-out comparison after ordinary moiré geometry and a full conventional contact/loss model are fixed. This note makes the comparison rule explicit. It supplies neither a UD source nor a physical forecast. The [primary Chen et al. PRL abstract](https://doi.org/10.1103/ly48-b4l2), checked 2 November 2026, reports detectable peaks at 21.8° and 38.2° with negligible load dependence, while the APS article and supplemental page require a subscription in the accessed path. No same-contact force vector, uncertainty matrix, detector threshold or measured complex loss was available. Those angles are exposed development targets, not a blind validation set.

## Typed inputs and exact likelihood identity

At registered angle, pressure, velocity, direction, temperature, contact and force-estimator settings, form an `n`-component **signed** force vector `Y` in one declared force unit. Freeze the ordinary honeycomb/moiré forecast `mu0∈R^n`, a fully specified added-port forecast `mu1∈R^n`, and a common symmetric positive-definite force-squared covariance `Sigma∈R^(n×n)` using independent calibration data. The covariance must include known baseline/contact calibration uncertainty and cross-row correlations under the declared Gaussian approximation. It is **not** a native UD metric. Controls at off-peak angles and other commensurate indices belong in the same preregistered vector; censored or threshold-only observations require a separate likelihood and are not coded as zero force.

Under the simple hypotheses `H0:Y~N(mu0,Sigma)` and `H1:Y~N(mu1,Sigma)`, put `delta=mu1-mu0`, `d²=delta^T Sigma^-1 delta`. For an observed vector `y`, the reduction in squared Mahalanobis misfit is

`Delta = (y-mu0)^T Sigma^-1 (y-mu0) - (y-mu1)^T Sigma^-1 (y-mu1) = 2 delta^T Sigma^-1(y-mu0)-d²`.

This identity is exact for the declared model. For `d>0`, `Delta` is normal with mean `-d²` under H0 or `+d²` under H1 and variance `4d²` under either. A one-sided size-`alpha` likelihood-ratio rule accepts the fixed H1 over H0 when `Delta > -d² + 2d z_(1-alpha)`; its H1 power is `Phi(d-z_(1-alpha))`. Thus 80% nominal power at 5% nominal false-alarm rate requires `d >= z_0.95+z_0.80 ≈ 2.4865`. These are **study-design numbers**, conditional on the Gaussian shared-covariance model; they are not a measured friction significance or a UD amplitude. `d=0` means the frozen forecasts are identical in this readout and no such test can distinguish them.

`forecast_gate.py` evaluates `d²` and `Delta` with exact rational arithmetic for rational inputs and reports the Gaussian tail in decimal form. Run `python3 experiments/graphite-signed-forecast-gate/forecast_gate.py --self-check` and `python3 experiments/graphite-signed-forecast-gate/forecast_gate.py experiments/graphite-signed-forecast-gate/SYNTHETIC_EXAMPLE.json`. The example is deliberately **SYNTHETIC**, with no graphite data. The script verifies positive definiteness but cannot prove that forecasts were frozen before exposure or that the covariance describes the apparatus.

## Release gate

Before unblinding a new dataset, archive the exact input revision/hash, unit definition, force sign convention, row order, contact identifiers, `mu0`, `mu1`, `Sigma`, `alpha`, model/data split, treatment of non-detections, and exclusion policy. The registered alternative must specify its K4-to-material source and dissipative response independently; post-exposure choices remain exploratory. Record calibration errors, repeats and instrumental drift; use a more complete correlated or censored likelihood if the simple Gaussian assumptions fail. Only an independently reviewed freeze and a genuinely new, blinded execution could support a confirmatory claim. A positive test alone cannot attribute the difference to UD when another physical model predicts the same `mu1`.
