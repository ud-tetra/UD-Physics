# Preparation and action-map contract — exploratory v1.0

This is an entry contract for a future candidate, not a completed apparatus protocol or preregistration. Known eta2 target exposure is explicit.

| Required object | Current status | Admission condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Counted transferred charge | External physical primitive granted as ideal e | Specify source, errors, gate cycle, destination, charge sign and traceability |
| Canonical port means | Assumed Q1=0,Q2=e,Phi=0 at the reference endpoint | Source-to-port response must establish those means, including current envelope and loading |
| State/covariance | OPEN | Supply Hilbert space, generator, density operator and finite moments; use two-mode covariance while coupled |
| Impedance selection | OPEN | Independent law or declared measured calibration of Z; identify whether calibration or universal selection is claimed |
| Quantum action scale chi | Added premise, no native derivation | Disclose the commutator and time generator; identifying chi=hbar is external input |
| Native unit identification | OPEN | Justify A_map=A0 without silently replacing support by electron count, photon count or a normalized ray |
| Energy readout | OPEN alternative conventions | Freeze total, excess or mean energy, reference vacuum/baseline, bandwidth, loading and uncertainties |
| Independent prediction | NOT_EXECUTED | Freeze correspondence and uncertainty propagation before a genuinely unused comparison |

Minimum rejection controls:

1. Vary Z with C->C/s,L->sL at the same native clock and g. A charge-only universal claim fails if it cannot distinguish this family.
2. Hold transferred charge fixed and vary source duration/envelope. Test final canonical state, not only integrated source current.
3. Keep the means fixed and vary covariance. An energy estimate must include or explicitly remove its contribution.
4. Compare displaced, squeezed and number states using the same declared baseline. An n=1 excitation is not mean-Q=e.
5. Trace any quantum Hall/Josephson/standard quantum input if proposed later; a definition or prior hbar normalization is not new evidence recovering hbar.

For a declared displacement scale A=e^2 Z, differential calibration sensitivity is dA/A=2 de/e+dZ/Z; this is a local identity, not a measured uncertainty budget. Charge count alone leaves Z unselected. Real pump error, correlation with Z, source noise and state-estimation uncertainty are not supplied and no numerical uncertainty claim is made.

Reopening condition: a source-bound, dimensionally complete constitutive/unit-preparation law that fixes the action-relevant combination, plus a compatible preparation/readout map. An independently calibrated analogue is useful under that narrower description even without universal selection.
