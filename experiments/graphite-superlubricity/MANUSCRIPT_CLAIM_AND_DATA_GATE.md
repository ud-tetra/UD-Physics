It reports weak load dependence at both paired peaks, but supplies no accessible numerical force-load table or uncertainty. The signed kinetic residual and its load derivative therefore remain **open**. The named DFT archive is independent computational work, not the 2026 experiment.

## Prediction and falsification contract

1. Independently validate a two-dimensional conventional PES over relevant separation and pressure, including transverse samples and finite edge/contact effects. The five proposed QE runs discriminate specified alternatives; the local Hessian additionally needs a declared Fourier or smoothness bound and an error covariance.
2. Measure pressure-, frequency-, and mode-resolved conservative and dissipative response using an identified force estimator. Calibrate $C,K,D_h$, the source weights $w$, the map $R$, contact area, and direct lateral/background damping without fitting the held-out paired-angle force traces.
3. Freeze the conventional force model and the restricted source rule $C=S(w)R$, including parameter covariance and a signed prediction for $F_{\mathrm{obs}}-F_{\mathrm{conventional}}$ and $\partial_P(F_{\mathrm{obs}}-F_{\mathrm{conventional}})$ at both angles. Mirror-related scalar symmetry alone is a conventional control, not a UD discriminator.
4. Compare held-out data. A zero or reversed source pattern, missing mode span, nonpassive sign under the additive passive ansatz, or failed signed residual prediction rejects or narrows this bridge. A positive fit after looking at the target force curves does not count as a prediction.
