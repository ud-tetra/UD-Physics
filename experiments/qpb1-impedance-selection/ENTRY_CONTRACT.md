# Electrical constitutive candidate entry contract — exploratory v1.0

No apparatus or dataset admitted; eta2 and the historical7/960 comparison are already exposed.

A new candidate must specify:

1. Native carrier, local neighborhoods, S4 action and canonical variables. Identify whether the current is support flow, winding transport or a newly assumed electrical quantity.
2. Charge map, including q0, sign, conserved quantity, source/channel and clock conversion. Winding number alone does not supply coulombs.
3. Positive energy/action functional, conjugate effort observable, constitutive parameters and their origin. Distinguish an imposed invariant metric from a selected one; covariance under S4 need not choose its coefficients.
4. Evolution, boundary/source conditions, preparation and measured readout. Check continuity, passivity/energy accounting and remaining scaling freedoms.
5. A source derivation or declared new hypothesis for lambda in `Z=lambda A0/q0²`. Unit convention alone is insufficient evidence of a physical constitutive rule.
6. Native unit-preparation identification and independent action/charge calibration, with traceability and uncertainty. Explicitly disclose any imported hbar, quantum resistance or vacuum-impedance identity.
7. A dimensionless consequence fixed without further adjustment and a comparison with the ordinary circuit/null model. Only unused independent evaluation can support an independent predictive-success claim.

Failing absolute-scale selection does not invalidate a useful analogue or dimensionless prediction. Record that narrower role and stop the universal-unit claim until an added input changes identifiability. Do not modify historical source/registry status to make a candidate appear accepted.
