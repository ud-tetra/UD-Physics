## 3. Exact dimensional obstruction and the added charge unit

Use independent dimensions `(M,L,T,Q)`, with Q denoting electric charge, and positive primitives B0,a0,tau0. Dimensional exponents are

\[
 [A_0]=(1,2,-1,0),\qquad [Z]=(1,2,-1,-2).
\]

The impedance formula follows from voltage times charge having energy units and current having charge/time units. Every monomial in B0,a0,tau0 has charge exponent zero, so no such monomial, even with arbitrary rational powers or dimensionless combinatorial factors, has impedance units. More generally, a unit-covariant expression using only those three dimensional inputs cannot acquire a missing charge dimension. This statement assumes there is no additional dimensional constant or hidden physical conversion in the input list.

Now **add** a positive charge unit q0. Dimensional covariance permits

\[
 Z=\lambda\,\frac{B_0a_0^2}{\tau_0q_0^2}
   =\lambda\frac{A_0}{q_0^2},\qquad
 \frac{A_{\rm map}}{A_0}=\lambda\left(\frac e{q_0}\right)^2.
\]

Lambda may depend on dimensionless native data. Dimensions alone select neither lambda nor the correspondence of q0 with a physical charge. Imposing `q0=e` and `lambda=1` makes the ratio one by construction; it is a declared map, not a second derivation of A0. Conversely, independently sourced A0, q0 and a native constitutive lambda would provide a testable impedance prediction. That positive route is not ruled out.

The earlier H=I native chain metric and frequency2 do not fix lambda in ohms. They normalize native amplitudes and rates. The [positive rescaling family](../qpb1-quantized-charge/README.md) changes Z and the action reference while retaining that native normalization, frequencies and coupling. No new clock or modular register is introduced here.

For a concrete compatibility check, introduce the **external** vacuum impedance `Z_vac=mu0*c` and physical alpha. NIST SP330 §2 and SP961/CODATA2022 supply the conventional identities, documented in [EXTERNAL_SOURCES.json](EXTERNAL_SOURCES.json). Combining them gives

\[
 Z_{\rm vac}=\frac{4\pi\alpha\hbar}{e^2}.
\]

If one additionally assumes `Z_port=zeta Z_vac` and `A0=e^2 Z_port`, then

\[
 \eta_A=4\pi\alpha\zeta.
\]

This is a **conditional compatibility equation**, not a native prediction. The extra port-to-vacuum ratio zeta is unselected. Borrowing the quantum-normalized expression for Z_vac imports hbar into an alleged independent reconstruction; independently measured impedance with disclosed traceability is a different calibration route.

As an exposed-target toy check only: adding `alpha=7/960` and `zeta=1` gives `eta_A=7pi/240`, which is not2 (pi<4 suffices). Making eta2 hold instead requires `zeta=480/(7pi)`. That inverse-selected value must not be relabeled a native result. This tests the compatibility of several added assumptions, and does not falsify UD or either ledger statement. Circuit impedance and vacuum wave impedance are different typed objects until an explicit map relates them. No CODATA numerical constant was fitted or used as new experimental data.
