## Incidence and dynamics
For a single domain undergoing genuine conforming subdivision and exact Whitney restriction P, isometry and d_new P=P d_old follow from equality of fields and exterior derivative on that domain. This does not hold without qualifications for single-field zero-extension on the union of two overlapping packets. Analytic canonical counterexample: old A nodal basis lambda=(1+x+y+z)/4 is 1/2 at p=(1,0,0). The B-exclusive cap vertex b=(1,1,-1) lies outside A, so its zero-extended nodal value is 0. On the oriented edge b->p the nodal coboundary is 1/2. But the open edge lies outside A (normal (-1,-1,1) has dot product <-1 there); the zero-extended old d lambda has integral zero on it. Therefore the naive shared-domain P does not commute with d already at degree zero. Interface trace/jump handling or separate labels is necessary. This proof is an analytic control, not a separately executed script assertion.

For fixed SPD M, cochain differential d, mass adjoint delta=M_low^{-1} d^T M_high, A=d-delta is M-skew. Therefore fixed-domain quadratic support is conserved; the converse claim that an isometric cochain map intertwines both d and delta is false. The exact script uses interval [0,1] split at h=1/3 with nodal and edge Whitney masses. It verifies P0^T N0 P0=M0, P1^T N1 P1=M1 and e P0=P1 d, yet delta_new P1 - P0 delta_old = (-3,2,-3/2)^T, nonzero. The symmetric split at 1/2 is also tested for metric and d identities. This interval is an independent mathematical counterexample to a general implication, not a native 3D event calculation. In the isometric commuting setting, P_low^dagger delta_new P_high=delta_old follows by adjointing d_new P_low=P_high d_old. Full intertwining additionally requires delta_new P_high lie in im(P_low), or zero orthogonal residual. Metric-skewness of post generator alone is not enough to transport old dynamics. If metric varies in time, fixed-M proof requires modification with dM/dt.
